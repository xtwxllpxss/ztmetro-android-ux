  `canShowTransient()` (WindowState.java:3948-3950) is
  (mAttrs.insetsFlags.behavior & BEHAVIOR_SHOW_TRANSIENT_BARS_BY_SWIPE) != 0, i.e.
  it is about the insets control target, not the shade. On an ordinary unlocked
  display that target is not a "show transient bars by swipe" window, so the
  else branch — the one with transferTouch() — is the normal path. That is why
  the defect reproduces on every top-edge swipe and why a late SystemUI hook can
  never win.
